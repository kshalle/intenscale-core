notice-retention term.  Any publication in any form derived from the use of
that software, or of any modification of it, must refer explicitly to the
original BSMBench package — including its official URL —
and cite:

> [1] L. Del Debbio, A. Patella, C. Pica, "Higher representations on the
> lattice: Numerical simulations. SU(2) with adjoint fermions",
> Phys. Rev. D81 (2010) 094503, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.81.094503
>
> [2] L. Del Debbio, B. Lucini, A. Patella, C. Pica, A. Rago, "Conformal
